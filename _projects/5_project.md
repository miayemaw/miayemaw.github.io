# Seoul-Style UAM Strategic Framework
**Duration:** 2022 - 2023 | **Funding:** Seoul Government
**Partners:** Seoul City Research Center, Hyundai UAM

### 01. Research Scope
This project focused on establishing the **Basic Plan for Seoul-Style Urban Air Mobility (UAM)**. The goal was to define how air taxis and eVTOLs will integrate into one of the world’s most densely populated cities, ensuring both operational efficiency and public safety.

### 02. Technical Contributions
* **Operational Scenarios:** Developed detailed UAM flight paths and operation protocols tailored to Seoul’s unique topography and airspace constraints.
* **High-Fidelity Simulation:** Built a digital twin of Seoul using **Unreal Engine** to validate takeoff/landing corridors and vertiport throughput.
* **System Validation:** Conducted feasibility studies on the integration of UAM with existing ground-based public transit systems.

### 03. Key Technologies
* **Engine:** Unreal Engine (Simulation Development)
* **Operations:** UAM Scenario Authoring & Conflict Resolution
* **Validation:** Urban Airspace Modeling & Infrastructure Planning

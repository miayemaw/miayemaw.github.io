# High-Fidelity UAV Digital Twin & VR Simulation Platform

### 01. Project Overview
**Focus:** 6-DOF Dynamics, Wearable Integration, and Multi-Platform Flight Validation

Developed a high-accuracy, real-time simulation ecosystem for UAVs using **Unity** and **Virtual Reality**. By bridging the gap between digital models and physical hardware, this platform enables safe, low-cost testing for complex flight algorithms and pilot training across multiple airframes.

---

### 02. Technical Contributions

* **High-Accuracy Modeling:** Developed a 6-DOF dynamics and sensor simulation framework supporting a diverse fleet, including **Octacopters, Hexacopters, Quadcopters, and Fixed-wing UAVs**.
* **Hardware-in-the-Loop:** Integrated **ArduPilot-based PID control** modules for seamless real-time closed-loop simulation.
* **Immersive Interface:** Leveraged **wearable display devices (VR)** to provide pilots with high-fidelity visual feedback and realistic training environments.
* **Automation:** Created scenario scripting tools to ensure 100% reproducibility in flight testing and algorithm validation.

---

### 03. Impact & Versatility
This project serves as a foundational **Digital Twin platform**. While designed for aerospace, the architecture is highly transferable to industrial robotics—specifically for trajectory validation, pick-and-place operations, and manufacturing workflow testing.
